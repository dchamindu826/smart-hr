# Privacy Policy for Smart HR

**Last Updated: October 16, 2026**

Smart HR ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by Lumi AI Solutions, values the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application (the "App").

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use Smart HR to manage your employee profile, attendance, and leave requests, we may collect the following data:

* **Account & Profile Information:** Your name, employee ID, company email address, job title, and department.
* **Attendance & Location Data:** When you clock in or clock out, our App records your GPS location strictly to verify on-site or authorized remote attendance. Location is **never** tracked in the background or continuously throughout the day.
* **Camera & Photos (Optional):** If enabled by your employer, camera access is used strictly for selfie verification during remote check-in, and photo library access is used if you choose to attach medical certificates or supporting documents for leave requests.
* **Device Information:** Basic device model, OS version, and app performance logs to ensure system reliability and security.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected solely for legitimate workplace administration purposes:
* Recording and verifying employee attendance (check-in / check-out times).
* Processing leave applications and approvals.
* Providing HR notifications and announcements.
* Maintaining internal security and compliance for your organization.

We do **not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to third-party advertisers.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security

All communication between the Smart HR mobile app and server systems is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols. Data is securely stored in enterprise-grade cloud databases with strict role-based access controls.

---

### 4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

Your information is only accessible by authorized HR administrators and managers within your employer's organization. We do not disclose personal information to external third parties unless legally required by applicable laws or regulatory authorities.

---

### 5. Your Rights and Data Retention

* You may review your personal profile and attendance history directly within the App.
* Your data is retained in accordance with your employer's HR retention policies and applicable labor laws.
* Upon termination of employment, account access is deactivated in coordination with your organization's HR department.
